# Sandeep Editorial Portfolio Brief

**Creative authority:** Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. Sandeep asked to use my opinion after specifying the intended content and the "student and developers magazine" direction.

## 1. Vibe

- User answer: "student and developers magazine."
- Authored decision: tactile editorial, credible, curious, clear. No neon, glass, gradient text, dashboards, or decorative terminal UI.

## 2. Scroll journey

- User answer: start with "Hey, I am Sandeep Manchinasetti", then skills, projects, internships, and the remaining portfolio.
- Authored sequence: title page, working profile and skills, selected work, career desk, awards and contact.

## 3. Energy curve

- Authored: calm recognition on the title page; focused momentum in skills; highest energy and largest visual span in selected work; grounded confidence in the career desk; warm, quiet resolve at contact.

## 4. Remembered moment

- User answer: visitors must remember Sandeep's skills and projects.
- Authored peak: a large asymmetric selected-work spread where the project labels physically become a readable project index as the visitor scrolls.

## 5. Signature move

- Authored: the **work index**. A vertical editorial margin records the current section and turns into a persistent project index. Hovering or focusing a project highlights its matching mark in the index.

## 6. Aesthetic range

- User answer: magazine.
- Authored decision: chaptered editorial, pale paper and ink with a single cobalt accent. It should feel authored by a student-developer, not generated for a generic AI product.

## 7. World structure

- Authored decision: distinct chapters. The visitor reads a portfolio feature, not a continuous cinematic world.

## 8. Assets

- Existing asset: `profile.jpg`.
- Project screenshots and new career data were not supplied. Project sections therefore use factual type-led case labels and existing repository/demo links, not invented imagery or metrics.

## Journey and feeling curve

| Chapter | Feeling | On-screen cause |
|---|---|---|
| Title page | Recognition | A direct introduction and a compact statement of focus. |
| Field notes | Curiosity | Portrait, skills and working approach are organized as a readable desk note. |
| Selected work | Confidence (peak) | The project index and asymmetric work spreads make proof the central visual event. |
| Career desk | Trust | Internship chronology is presented as clear editorial entries, not badges. |
| Open line | Resolve | A quiet, direct invitation and contact path end the reading experience. |

## Tell-someone sentence

"It is the portfolio where Sandeep's projects read like the cover story, not a grid of AI-generated cards."

## Grammar and score

Grammar: **Chaptered editorial**. It fits a student-developer magazine, keeps the title page calm, and lets evidence read as chapters rather than a product landing page.

| Beat | Device | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Recognition | Title page + reveal | The introduction is immediately legible, with no media competing above the fold. |
| Field notes | Flow + entry reveal | Skills and profile need a reading rhythm. |
| Selected work | Custom work-index + reveal | The primary proof deserves the largest visual chapter. |
| Career desk | Flow timeline | Dates and roles are easier to trust when read linearly. |
| Open line | Inverted colophon | The contact action resolves as the last editorial line. |
